ASCII & hex workbench

Every lab here comes back to the same literacy: bytes, their hex, and the characters they stand for. This is the bench for that — the ASCII table, a live hexdump -C / xxd, the reverse (hex → text, like xxd -r), and a few classic ciphers for whispering over the wire. Everything runs locally in the page; nothing is sent anywhere.
