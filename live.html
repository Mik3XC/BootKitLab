chat over the wire · Morpheus

The OS already speaks over a serial line (COM1). morpheus.asm reads your keypresses with BIOS int 0x14 and replies on screen — a guided conversation across the wire. This panel simulates that link so you can try it here.

COM1 · 44444 — idle
What’s real, what’s simulated. The bytes, the int 0x14 read loop and morpheus.asm (368 bytes) are real and on disk. The replies here are scripted in the page. A real chat needs three things this page stands in for: a socket (the TCP port QEMU opens on 44444), a server (morpheus.asm, reading the wire), and a client (nc localhost 44444, your end). Wire those three together and the conversation is live metal.
